## First Section

Use level-two headings (`##`) to break the article into sections. Write your main content in plain paragraphs. You can emphasize text with *italics* or **bold**, and link to sources like [this](https://example.com).

## Second Section

- **Key point one**: A short explanation of the first idea.
- **Key point two**: A short explanation of the second idea.

1. First step.

## Closing Section
